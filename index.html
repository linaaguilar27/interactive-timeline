2026 – Drafting Phase

  • January: Analysis of consultation results and Working Group (WG) applicants.
  • February:
    • Secretariat updates the Draft Terms of Reference (ToR) based on consultation results.
    • WG applicants and experts are shared with the Policy and Steering Group and Policy and Standards Committee for information.
    • WG seats for biodiversity expert, natural forest manager and researcher are open for applicants .
  • April:
    • The final ToR and Working Group composition are approved at BM 105.
  • May:
    • The first PCI Working Group kick-off call takes place.
    • Working Group is announced
    • Consultative Forums are launched
    • Final ToR, Consultation Report and Engagement Plan are published
  • June:
    • First in-person Working Group meeting. Discussion of the roles and responsibilities of the different WG bodies, making PCI outcome orientated, structure, and modularity of the PCI.
    • Consultative Forums are launched. Sign Up Here
  • July–November: Bi-weekly virtual Working Group meetings are held to discuss the key topics outlined in the ToR. Consultative forums are held to gather feedback on key topics to inform and support Working Group discussions.
  • December: Second PCI Working Group meeting to align on key topics in the ToR and the development of the PCI draft in preparation for public consultation in 2027.

2027 – Drafting Continues

Continued development and refinement of drafts based on public input.
